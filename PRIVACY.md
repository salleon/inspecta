# Inspecta privacy policy

_Last updated: 10 November 2026_

Inspecta is an app for recording site inspections. This policy covers the iPhone, Android and web versions.

## What Inspecta collects

Nothing. Inspecta has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no servers. The developer cannot see anything you enter into the app.

## What stays on your device

Everything you create in Inspecta is stored only on your device: sites, findings, notes, photos, test results and settings. It leaves your device only when you choose to export, share or back it up, and it goes only where you send it.

## Permissions

- **Camera:** to take photos of findings.
- **Photo library:** to add existing photos to a finding, and to save a copy of the photos you take into an album for each site.
- **Microphone:** to measure sound level during tests. The measurement happens on your device; audio is not recorded or stored.

## Deleting your data

Delete a site or finding in the app to remove it, or uninstall the app to remove everything.

## Changes

Any change to this policy will be published on this page.
