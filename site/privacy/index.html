Privacy policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

In short: Inspecta collects no data. Everything you create stays on your device.

Inspecta is an app for recording site inspections. This policy covers the iPhone, Android and web versions of the app.

What Inspecta collects

Nothing. Inspecta has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no servers. The developer cannot see anything you enter into the app.

What stays on your device

Everything you create in Inspecta is stored only on your device: sites, findings, notes, photos, test results and settings. It leaves your device only when you choose to export, share or back it up, and it goes only where you send it, such as an email, a messaging app or cloud storage you pick.

Inspecta's data is left out of your phone's automatic backups (Google's Android backup and iCloud), so use Back up in the app to keep a copy. When you set up a new Android phone by copying your old phone across, Android may move Inspecta's data straight to the new phone; it doesn't go through the cloud. The copies of photos Inspecta saves to your photo library are handled by your phone's photo app, which may sync them if you've set it to.

Permissions

  • Camera: to take photos of findings.
  • Photo library: to add existing photos to a finding, and to save a copy of the photos you take into an album for each site.
  • Microphone: to measure sound level during tests. The measurement happens on your device; audio is not recorded or stored.

You can turn any of these off in your phone's settings. The rest of the app keeps working.

App stores

If you install Inspecta from Google Play or the App Store, Google or Apple handle the download and updates under their own privacy policies. They may share anonymous crash and usage statistics with developers if you've allowed that in your phone's settings. Inspecta itself adds nothing to this.

Children

Inspecta is a work tool, not made for children, and collects no data from anyone.

Deleting your data

Delete a site or finding in the app to remove it, or uninstall the app to remove everything.

Changes

Any change to this policy will be published on this page, with a new date at the top.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at github.com/salleon/inspecta/issues.
