Inspecta

Site inspections, one tap at a time

Inspecta is a fast, camera-first app for fire safety site inspections. Take a photo and it becomes a finding; add a note and you're straight back at the camera for the next one. When you're done, send the whole site as a report.

What it does

  • Camera-first capture: every photo starts a new finding, with extra photos, notes, levels and locations.
  • Defect types, ESR categories and suggested rectifications for each finding.
  • Reports as PDF and Excel, or every photo in one zip, with time and date stamps.
  • Works completely offline. Everything is kept on your phone, with no account to create.

Coming soon to Google Play and the App Store for Android and iPhone.

Support

Found a problem, or have a question or idea? Open an issue on GitHub and include your phone model and the version shown in Inspecta's Settings.

Privacy

Inspecta collects nothing: no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no servers. Read the full privacy policy.
